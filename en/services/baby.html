What is included

  • Care at your place, meals and bedtime as you instruct

How it works

  1. You describe what you need and pick a slot.
  2. The request goes to vetted professionals for that trade, or to the one you chose.
  3. The first to accept comes; you follow the job and chat in the app.
  4. Payment is secured and only charged once you have approved the job.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose the sitter?

Yes. For childcare you see who is available and pick one: the request goes to that person only.

From what age?

You and the sitter agree before she accepts: the children’s ages and your instructions are in the request.

What about a late evening?

Put the end time in the request, so the sitter accepts knowing exactly what she takes on.
